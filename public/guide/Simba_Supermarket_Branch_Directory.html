Simba Supermarket Ltd

Branch Directory & Location Guide — Kigali, Rwanda

April 2026  |  11 Branches in Operation
Simba Supermarket Ltd is one of Rwanda's leading retail chains, established in 2007 and officially launched on August 8, 2008. With 11 branches spread across Kigali's three districts — Nyarugenge, Gasabo, and Kicukiro — Simba offers a wide range of products including food, furniture, clothing, stationery, cosmetics, and toys.
